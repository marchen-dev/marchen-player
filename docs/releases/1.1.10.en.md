# Marchen 1.1.10

## Playback

- Hold the Right Arrow key to play temporarily at 3× speed. Release it to return to your previous speed, or tap it to skip ahead 5 seconds.
- Find the keyboard shortcuts in Playback preferences in the player settings.
- Fixed playback stalls that could occur at high speeds in the compatibility engine.

## Danmaku

- Adjust the danmaku display area with a slider. The font size slider now places the default 100% setting in the center.
- When you add danmaku from a link, the sidebar scrolls as needed to show the import progress, episode picker, or result.
