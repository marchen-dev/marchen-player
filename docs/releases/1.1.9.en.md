# Marchen 1.1.9

## Player settings

- Redesigned danmaku and subtitle settings: font size, opacity and danmaku speed are now sliders that can be reset to default, and the display area uses a segmented control.
- Danmaku settings are grouped into Match, Display, Appearance, and Sources and rules; subtitle settings into Subtitle track and Adjustments.
- Removed the on-screen density option. Up to 120 danmaku are shown at once.
- Fixed the "Very slow" and "Slow" danmaku speeds being in the wrong order.

## Updates and media sources

- On Windows and Linux AppImage, new versions are downloaded in the background. You'll be notified when one is ready and can choose when to install.
- When adding a media source, you can leave out `http://` in the server address.
